About Holistic Life

Holistic Life is a small guide site about home workouts, small-space gym setups and easy healthy meals. It is written for busy people who want practical help they can use today, without a lot of jargon.

Who runs this site

This site is run by a registered nurse. Nurses spend their days helping people make sense of health information, and that habit shapes every page here: keep it plain, keep it useful, and be honest about what is and is not known.

How the guides are written

  • General information only. Guides share ideas and options. They do not diagnose, treat or replace advice from your own clinician.
  • Sources are listed. When a guide states a health fact, it links to a reliable source such as the CDC, NIH, the American Heart Association, the USDA, Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic or Harvard Health.
  • Product categories, not hype. We describe what to look for in a type of product, so you can choose well in any store. We do not promise results from any item.
  • No promises about your body. You will not find weight loss claims, calorie targets or before and after stories here. We write about energy, strength, habits and feeling better.
  • Dates are shown. Each guide shows when it was last updated.
  • Drafted with AI help, held to these standards. Guides are drafted with the help of AI tools and checked against the sources listed. The nurse who runs the site sets the standards and has the final say on what is published.

How the site makes money

Some guides contain affiliate links. If you buy through one of them, Holistic Life may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That income pays for the time it takes to research and write the guides. Every guide with affiliate links says so at the top, and the full details are on the disclosure page.

You can also read the guides without clicking anything. Each one is meant to be useful on its own.
