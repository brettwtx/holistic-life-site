Christmas Gifts for Coworkers That Get Used

Quick answer

  • Christmas gifts for coworkers work best when they are small, useful and the same for everyone.
  • For a whole team, split a pack: good pens, unscented hand cream, lip balm, sealed snacks or badge reels.
  • For one person or a Secret Santa name, a leakproof mug, a water bottle, a lunch bag or a slim charger needs no size.
  • Skip strong scents, alcohol, joke gifts and anything about dieting.

The best Christmas gifts for coworkers are small, useful and the same for everyone. Nobody has to wonder what the gift means, and nobody feels left out. A pen that works or a snack in the break room does more than a fancy present that ends up in a drawer.

This is a nurse-run site, and the list works for an office, a school or a hospital unit. It also works for a Secret Santa swap. If you want coworker gift ideas for a whole team, split a pack. If you drew one name, give one useful thing.

Three rules for a gift a coworker will use

  1. It gets used up or used daily. Pens, lip balm, snacks and a mug all get picked up again and again.
  2. It is safe for a shared space. Nothing with a strong scent. If it is food, it should be sealed.
  3. It is the same for everyone, or it stays inside the limit the team agreed. Nobody should feel they got the smaller gift.

Secret Santa ideas for work

You drew the name of someone you barely know. Stay inside the limit the team set, and pick from the ideas that need no size, such as the mug, the bottle, the charger or the lunch bag. None of them needs a guess at taste. Many people already drink coffee or water at work, so these get used. Add a short note with your gift, such as "Happy holidays, glad we work together." That is enough, and it keeps things easy for both of you.

Gifts for nurse coworkers and healthcare teams

Many units limit fragrance, so unscented matters most. Food should be sealed, and there should be enough for night shift and weekend staff too. Badge reels and pens are used on every shift, so they are safe picks for any unit. Check the unit's or hospital's own gift rules before you drop anything off.

For more ideas, see our guide to gifts for nurses. For a gift the whole unit shares, see our thank-you basket ideas for a nursing unit. If your coworker works nights, our night shift gift basket ideas will help.

What to skip

  • Strongly scented candles and lotions. Many workplaces ask people to avoid scents, and a strong one fills a shared room.
  • Alcohol. Skip it unless you know the person well and the workplace allows it.
  • Joke gifts. They get a laugh once and then sit in a drawer.
  • Anything about dieting or body size. It can feel personal, even when you mean well.
  • Homemade food with no ingredient list. It is kind, but not everyone can eat everything. Add a label, or choose sealed snacks.
  • Clothes in a guessed size. A wrong size is a gift that cannot be used.

Add a note

A short, specific line of thanks is the part people keep. Say what they did that helped you, such as covering a shift or showing you how a job is done. For more small ideas, see our stocking stuffers for fitness lovers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for coworkers?

Pick something small, useful and the same for everyone, such as good pens, unscented hand cream, lip balm or sealed snacks. Split a pack across the team so nobody is left out.

What do you buy for a Secret Santa at work?

Choose one useful thing that needs no size and no guess at taste, such as a leakproof mug, a water bottle or a slim charger. Stay inside the limit the team set, and add a short note.

What should you not give a coworker?

Skip strongly scented candles and lotions, alcohol, joke gifts, anything about dieting or body size, and clothes in a guessed size. Homemade food needs an ingredient label, or choose sealed snacks.
