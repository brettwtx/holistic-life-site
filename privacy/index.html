Privacy

We keep this simple because the site is simple.

What this site does not do

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • It has no accounts, forms or comment sections, so we do not collect your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • It loads its fonts from its own files, so your browser does not contact a font service when you read a page.

Hosting

The company that hosts this site may keep ordinary technical logs, such as the pages requested and the network address of the request. That is common for any website, and those logs are handled under the host's own policies.

Links to other sites

Many guides link to stores such as Amazon and to other sites such as Pinterest. When you follow a link, you leave Holistic Life and the other site's privacy policy applies. Retailers and affiliate programs often use cookies to track a click through to a purchase, so that a commission can be credited. We do not see that information.

The "Save to Pinterest" buttons open Pinterest in a new tab. Nothing is shared with Pinterest until you choose to tap one.

Contact

If you have a question about this page, reach us through our Pinterest profile.

Changes

If we ever change how the site works in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page first.

Some links on this site are affiliate links, so Holistic Life may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.
