Affiliate and Health Disclosure

This page explains, in plain English, how Holistic Life earns money and what you should keep in mind about the health information on this site.

Affiliate links

Some pages on this site contain affiliate links. An affiliate link is a normal link to a store or service that carries a tracking code. If you click it and then buy something, Holistic Life may earn a small commission. You pay the same price either way.

Pages with affiliate links say so near the top, before the first link, and the buttons that lead to a store are marked "(paid link)".

How we choose what to recommend

We recommend types of products and describe what to look for, rather than pushing a single brand or model. Earning a commission does not change what a guide says. If a product type is not worth buying for a given purpose, the guide says so.

Links to stores are often search links, so the products you see, and their prices and availability, are set by the store and can change at any time. We do not control them.

Health disclaimer

The guides on Holistic Life are general information, not medical advice. They are not a diagnosis or a treatment plan, and they do not replace care from your own doctor, nurse or other clinician.

Check with your own clinician before you start a new exercise or eating plan, especially if you have a health condition, are pregnant, or are recovering from an injury. Stop an activity and get medical help if you feel unwell.

If you think you may be having a medical emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
